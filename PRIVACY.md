# Melo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 2, 2026_

Melo is a client for [Music Player Daemon (MPD)](https://www.musicpd.org) servers. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## What the app stores on your device

- The addresses, names, and optional passwords of the MPD servers you add. Passwords are kept in the device Keychain. This information never leaves your device except to connect to the server you configured.
- Playback preferences and settings.

## Network access

Melo connects only to the MPD servers you configure. It uses local network access and Bonjour to discover MPD servers on your network. It does not contact any other server, and it has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind.

## Third parties

Melo contains no third-party SDKs that collect data and does not share data with anyone.

## Children

Melo does not collect data from anyone, including children.
